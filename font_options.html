licensing + technical

  • Literata — OFL · JetBrains Mono — OFL. Both variable.
  • Source Serif 4 — OFL · IBM Plex Mono — OFL. Both variable.
  • Newsreader — OFL (Production Type) · Commit Mono — OFL (Eigil Nikolajsen). Both variable.
  • Instrument Serif — OFL (Rodrigo Fuenzalida) · Geist Mono — OFL (Vercel). Geist is variable; Instrument Serif ships Regular + Italic only — good trade for its display punch.
  • Public Sans — OFL (U.S. Web Design System) · JetBrains Mono — OFL. Both variable.

how to read the ratings
